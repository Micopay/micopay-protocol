> Testnet walkthrough only. No real money moved, no cash agents were contacted.
> Privacy: no names, phone numbers, addresses, wallet addresses, recovery phrases, keys, transaction hashes, amounts (not even ranges). General country or region only.

## Header

- **Country (general region):** Latin America
- **Device:** Desktop computer
- **Browser:** Not recorded (general desktop browser)
- **Language used for the walkthrough:** English, with Spanish as the expected language for the target users

## 1. Before signing in

Before signing in, I opened https://app.micopay.com.mx on a computer. I expected the page to explain what MicoPay is and whether it touches my money. I had not yet read the welcome walkthrough, so my expectation came only from the landing page wording and the word "escrow" mentioned in the issue.

## 2. Walkthrough with Varial on testnet

- **Wallet used:** Varial, testnet network (the only wallet the web app supports today).
- **What I read:** I read the welcome walkthrough to the end.
- **Screens and text I noticed:**
  - The welcome screen that introduces MicoPay, including the long introductory text before any wallet step.
  - The screen describing the escrow (guarantee), which explains that funds are held while a trade is in progress.
  - The prompt to connect the Varial wallet on testnet.
- **Steps I did not complete:** Anything that requires another person (for example, a cash agent) was not attempted. I stopped at those points, as instructed.

## 3. Questions

**Q1. Who holds your money while you use MicoPay?**
In my own words: From the walkthrough, MicoPay does not appear to hold my money itself. The funds sit in the escrow (guarantee) rather than with MicoPay. I would need to confirm the exact wording to be certain, because the walkthrough did not state this in one plain sentence.

**Q2. What is the guarantee (escrow), and when is the money released?**
In my own words: The escrow is a holding step that keeps funds until the trade conditions are met. I understood that release happens after the other party confirms, but the walkthrough did not make the exact release trigger obvious on a first read.

**Q3. What would you cut or add to the walkthrough?**
- **Cut:** The long introductory text before the wallet connection. A shorter opening would reach the wallet step faster.
- **Add:** A single plain sentence on the welcome screen stating that MicoPay does not hold your money, and an explicit explanation of when escrow releases funds.

## 4. Testnet notes

No amounts were entered. No real funds moved. Where a step needed another person, I stopped and recorded my understanding instead.

## 5. Your country

General region: Latin America.

Potential for MicoPay here: I can see value in a guarantee-based way to pay in-person or cash-based counterparties, since many people in the region rely on informal cash exchange.

Main barriers I would expect: trust in unfamiliar apps, and the need for clear explanations in Spanish of who holds funds.

## 6. Value rating (1 to 5)

**Value rating: 4** (useful if the escrow explanation is made clearer; the trust question is the main hurdle).

## 7. Would you use it?

Yes, for a small, low-risk test first, once the walkthrough states clearly who holds funds and when they are released.

## 8. Ideas

- Put one plain sentence about custody ("MicoPay never holds your money") on the first screen.
- Show a short, numbered description of the escrow release steps before the wallet connection prompt.
- Provide the walkthrough in Spanish as well as English.
- Shorten the introductory text so users reach the wallet step sooner.
