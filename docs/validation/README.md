# Product validation — MicoPay web app

> **What this is:** short hands-on tasks where contributors try the MicoPay web app on **testnet** and tell us, in their own words, whether they **understand** it, whether they **see potential for it in their country**, what **confuses** them, and what **ideas** they have. MicoPay reviews every answer and writes the aggregate conclusions in [`SYNTHESIS.md`](./SYNTHESIS.md).

## How to take part

1. **Apply** to one of the `MP-V…` issues through the Drips bot. One assignee per issue.
2. Once assigned, **copy [`TEMPLATE.md`](./TEMPLATE.md) to `responses/<ISSUE-ID>.md`** (for example `responses/MP-V1.md`) and fill it in.
3. Open a **PR that only adds that file.** PRs that edit any other file are not merged.

## What you need

- A browser on a computer or a phone.
- The **Varial** wallet, switched to **testnet**. It is the only wallet the web app supports today.
- Nothing else. The app is a **technical beta on testnet**: no real money moves, and there are **no cash agents online yet**. Each issue tells you how far to go; when a step needs another person, stop there and tell us what you understood.

The app: **https://app.micopay.com.mx**

## The order matters

- **Step 1 is written first,** before you sign in or read anything else. We want to know whether the first screen explains the product on its own.
- Do not edit step 1 afterwards. If you change your mind, say it in step 2; that is exactly what we want to learn.

## Privacy first

> ⚠️ No real names, phone numbers, addresses, wallet addresses, private keys, recovery phrases, documents, receipts, transaction hashes or financial details. **We do not ask for any amounts of money**, not even ranges. A fee **percentage** is fine. Share only a **general country or region** and **your own** experience. Screenshots of the app are fine if they show no wallet address. English or Spanish.

**Never share your wallet's recovery phrase or secret key with anyone, including us.** MicoPay will never ask for it.

## First person only

Answer about **yourself**: what you understood, what you live or see where you live, what you would do. Do not survey other people, and do not write generalities ("people in X usually…") or text produced by a tool. It is fine to say "I don't know" or "this does not happen where I live".

## When a PR is accepted

- It follows the template and the privacy rules.
- Step 1 reads like a first impression.
- Answers point at concrete parts of the app: a screen, a button, a sentence.
- If not, we request changes.

## What we do with the answers

MicoPay reads every response and keeps an aggregate, anonymized synthesis in [`SYNTHESIS.md`](./SYNTHESIS.md): what people understood from the first screen, which misunderstandings repeat, where people got stuck, the value rating, the potential people see in their countries, and the ideas that repeat. It never copies personal data.

This is a convenience sample of Drips contributors: a directional, qualitative signal, not a representative study.
